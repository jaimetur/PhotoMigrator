    What if the cloud service fails?

    We refund the full amount automatically and delete your data. You never pay for a task that did not finish.

    Can I cancel a cloud task?

    Yes. Before your task starts you get a full refund. Once it is running it can be stopped, but it is not refunded.

    Which credentials do I give the cloud service?

    Only the ones of your destination: an Immich API key, a NextCloud app password, a Synology account or a Google Photos token. Create them just for the task and revoke them when it ends.

    How do I get a processed Takeout?

    You download it in ZIP parts of up to 50 GB that you can resume. The link works for 7 days and you pay only when the result is ready.

    What it does

    From a messy export to a tidy library

    Every task shows only the options it needs, explains each one in plain language and shows the exact command before anything runs.

    Automatic Migration

    Your whole library, moved in one run

    Choose where your photos are and where they should go. PhotoMigrator downloads and uploads in parallel, recreates every album on the target and shows a live dashboard while it works.

    • Copy or move assets, with albums and non-album photos
    • Retries, upload size limits and timeouts for big libraries
    • Filter by date, type, country, city or person
    • Optional album name clean-up and consolidation on the target
    Google & iCloud Takeout

    Exports that make sense again

    Takeout exports scatter dates and albums into side files. PhotoMigrator reads them, writes the real date, GPS and camera data back into every photo and rebuilds your albums.

    • Dates and locations restored from JSON sidecars or Photo Details.csv
    • Albums as links to one copy of each photo, so nothing is duplicated
    • Year or year/month folders, duplicate removal and dated album names
    • People labels kept for Immich, iCloud Memories rebuilt as albums
    Photo libraries

    Tidy up any library, safely

    Pick an action from a visual menu: upload or download albums, rename them by pattern, merge duplicates or clean up empty albums. Destructive actions are clearly marked and ask before they run.

    • Upload and download albums or the whole library
    • Rename albums with text, wildcards or regular expressions
    • Remove duplicate assets, keeping the best copy and its people
    • Merge, consolidate and clean up albums

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is PhotoMigrator really free?

    Yes. It is open source under the GPL-3.0 license, with no paid tiers, accounts or ads. You can read every line of code on GitHub.

    Will I keep my albums, dates and locations?

    Yes. Albums are recreated on the target, and when you start from a Google or iCloud Takeout the original date, GPS location and camera data are written back into each photo before it is migrated.

    Can I migrate out of Google Photos?

    Since April 2025 Google no longer lets third-party apps read your whole Google Photos library. Request a Google Takeout of your photos and use it as the source: PhotoMigrator fixes it and migrates it. Google Photos still works as a target.

    Where do my photos go?

    Only where you send them. PhotoMigrator runs on your own computer, NAS or server and talks directly to the services you configure. In the Web Interface, passwords and API keys are stored encrypted for each user.

    Can I use more than one account?

    Yes. Each service supports up to three accounts, so you can also migrate between two accounts of the same service, for example from one Immich user to another.

    What happens if a migration is interrupted?

    Uploads that fail are retried, and assets that keep failing are kept aside and listed so you can review them. You can run the migration again with the same settings by copying its command.

    Which interface should I use?

    On a NAS or server, the Web Interface with Docker. On your own computer, the desktop app. Over SSH, the terminal interface. For automation, the command line with PhotoMigrator --help.
