{{ operator }} is responsible for the personal data processed by PhotoMigrator Cloud. Contact: {{ contact }}.

2. What data we process

3. Why we process it

Only to provide the service you request: to quote, run and deliver your tasks, to send you emails about them and to keep the accounting records the law requires. The legal basis is the contract you accept when you create a task, and our legal obligations for payment records.

4. How long we keep it

  • Task files and credentials: until the task ends, fails or is canceled, or until the end of the download period ({{ download_days }} days).

  • Each task runs in its own isolated folder that no other user can reach.

  • PhotoMigrator is open source, so anyone can check what it does.
